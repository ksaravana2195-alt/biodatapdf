A marriage biodata is a one-page profile that families share when looking for a match. A clear format helps the other family understand you quickly.

## 1. Personal details
Full name, date of birth, time and place of birth, height, complexion, blood group, marital status, mother tongue and diet.

## 2. Religion and community
Religion, caste or community and sect. Hindu families usually add gotra, rashi, nakshatra and manglik status. Muslim families add sect and biradari. Sikh families often mention Keshdhari or Amritdhari status.

## 3. Education and career
Highest qualification, college, occupation, employer, work location and annual income. Add income only if you are comfortable sharing it.

## 4. Family details
Father’s and mother’s names and occupation, number of siblings and their marital status, native place and family type.

## 5. Contact details
A phone or WhatsApp number of a parent or guardian, email and current address.

## 6. Photograph
Use a recent, clear, well-lit photo. A large photo on the page works better than a tiny passport-size picture.

## Tips
Keep it to one page, use a readable font, check spellings and save as PDF so formatting never changes.
