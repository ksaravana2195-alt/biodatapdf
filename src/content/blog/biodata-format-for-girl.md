A bride's biodata should feel warm and respectful while still giving the key facts.

## Sections to include
1. **Personal details:** name, date and time of birth, place of birth, height, complexion, diet.
2. **Religion and community:** caste or community, gotra, rashi and nakshatra if needed.
3. **Education and career:** qualification, college, job or business, work city.
4. **Family details:** parents' names and occupation, siblings, native place.
5. **Hobbies:** three or four real interests such as painting, music, cooking or dance.
6. **Contact:** a parent's or guardian's number.

## Short sample
"I am a product designer who loves art and classical dance. I am independent, caring and close to my family. I am looking for an understanding partner with a positive outlook."

## Safety tips for sharing
- Share your biodata only with families you are in touch with.
- Consider giving a parent's phone number instead of your own.
- Do not add Aadhaar, bank or exact address details until you know the family well.
