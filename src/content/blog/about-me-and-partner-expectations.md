The About Me section is the only place in a biodata where your personality shows. Keep it short, honest and positive.

## About Me: a simple formula
Write two or three sentences covering your nature, your values and what you enjoy. For example: “Down-to-earth and family-oriented. I enjoy travelling and cooking, and I value honesty and respect.”

## Partner expectations: be clear, not demanding
List two or three things that truly matter, such as education, family values or lifestyle. Avoid long lists of conditions.

Example: “Looking for an understanding and well-educated partner who respects family values and has a positive outlook.”

## Things to avoid
Exaggeration, long paragraphs, negative comments about past relationships and strict conditions on looks or income.

## Hobbies
Add 3 to 5 genuine hobbies such as reading, music, cricket or painting. They help start conversations between families.
