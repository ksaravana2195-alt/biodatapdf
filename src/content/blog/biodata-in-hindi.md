Many families prefer a biodata with Hindi headings. These are the words you will use most.

## Common Hindi terms
- **Naam:** name
- **Janm tithi / Janm samay / Janm sthan:** date, time and place of birth
- **Lambai:** height
- **Varn / Rang:** complexion
- **Gotra, Rashi, Nakshatra:** as in English
- **Shaikshanik yogyata:** education
- **Vyavsay / Naukri:** business or job
- **Vaarshik aay:** annual income
- **Pita / Mata:** father and mother
- **Bhai / Behen:** brother and sister
- **Sampark:** contact

## How to make it
On BiodataPDF you can type your answers in Hindi using any Hindi keyboard. The blessing line at the top also supports Devanagari, for example "Shri Ganeshaya Namah". The PDF keeps the Hindi text sharp.

## Tip
Keep Hindi and English consistent. If you type names in Hindi, type cities and employers in Hindi too so the page looks uniform.
