A groom's biodata should be clear, honest and easy to scan. Use this order.

## Sections to include
1. **Personal details:** name, date and time of birth, place of birth, height, complexion, blood group, marital status.
2. **Religion details:** caste or community, gotra, rashi and nakshatra if your family follows kundli matching.
3. **Education and career:** qualification, college, job title, company, work city and annual income.
4. **Family details:** father's and mother's names and occupation, siblings, native place, family type.
5. **Contact:** a guardian's phone number and address.
6. **Expectations:** two or three lines about the partner you are looking for.

## Short sample
"I am a software engineer working in Bengaluru. I enjoy cricket and travelling, and I value honesty and family. I am looking for an educated, caring partner who respects both career and family."

## Common mistakes
- Writing a very long About Me.
- Using an old or blurry photo.
- Leaving out contact details.
- Copying expectations that do not match your own lifestyle.
