Your photo is the first thing people notice. A good one can make a biodata feel trustworthy and complete.

## Choose a recent, natural photo
Use a photo taken within the last year, with a natural smile and your face clearly visible.

## Lighting and background
Stand facing a window or sit in soft daylight. Use a plain background and avoid strong shadows or filters.

## Clothing
Wear simple, comfortable traditional or formal clothes in a colour that suits you. Avoid sunglasses, caps and group photos.

## Framing
A head-and-shoulders or half-length portrait works best. On BiodataPDF you can crop to the exact frame of your template so the face stays centred.

## Size and quality
Upload the original photo instead of a screenshot. Our crop tool keeps it sharp so it prints well on A4.
